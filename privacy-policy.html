Barakah Beads – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Barakah Beads is a tasbih (dhikr counter) app made by Samisa Studios. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything you count stays on your phone. We don't collect, store, or share your tasbihs, history or settings.

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • The tasbihs you create (names, Arabic text, counts, bead and goal settings)
  • Your daily counting history and streaks
  • Your settings (design, appearance, vibration, reminder time, and similar choices)
  • Whether you have unlocked Barakah Beads Pro

This data never leaves your phone. We have no servers and cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. Your phone's own backup feature (for example, Android backup to your Google account) may include app data, depending on your phone settings; that backup is controlled by you and your phone's provider, not by us.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No data sold or shared with anyone
  • No internet connection is needed to count. The app only goes online for purchases and app updates, described below.

Purchases (Pro and tips)

Barakah Beads Pro and the optional tips are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card or payment details.

To unlock or restore Pro, the app asks Google Play which purchases belong to the Google account on your phone. The answer is kept on your phone only. Like every app sold on Google Play, we can see basic order details in our Google Play account (such as the order number, the product, the price and the country), which we use only for refunds and support.

App updates

To get small improvements and fixes without a full store update, the app checks our update service, run by Expo (Expo Privacy Policy). This check sends technical details such as the app version and your phone's platform, not your tasbihs, history or any personal information.

Home screen widget

If you add the Barakah Beads widget to your home screen, it shows your latest dhikr and counts from the same data stored on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used only for the daily reminder you turn on in Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Vibration: used for a short tap feeling when you count. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Volume keys (optional): if you turn on "Count with volume keys", the app reads volume key presses only while the counter is open, to count. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us from the app (to ask a question, send feedback, or request a design), your email app opens with a message that includes the app version and your phone's operating system version, to help us reply. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it only to answer you. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
